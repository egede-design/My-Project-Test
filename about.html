About Destiny

A community where every learner belongs

We bring students, families, and teachers together to create a caring place where children can grow with confidence and purpose.

Our approach

Growing curious, capable, and kind learners

At Destiny Foundation School, learning is built on strong relationships. Our teachers get to know each student, encourage questions, and make room for different strengths and ways of learning.

We work in partnership with families so students feel supported at school and at home. Together, we help them build the knowledge, character, and confidence to take their next steps.

A safe community

Every student deserves to feel welcomed, respected, and encouraged to do their best.

Learning with purpose

Meaningful learning helps students connect new ideas to the world around them.

Families as partners

Open communication helps us support each learner and celebrate their progress.
